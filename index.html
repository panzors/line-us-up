Why I Built This

Keep your data entirely yours, no apps, no database. Just you! This is hosted on github and the information is on your browser's local storage.

How to use

Click on the stars for what you're into. Second click makes it into a must see for you. Export your list and or import your friends list. Export as calendar invite and import it on your phone or use the "Party mode" option. Printable option is also available.

The main view is generally best used on desktop. Party mode is best kept as a webpage loaded on your phone.
