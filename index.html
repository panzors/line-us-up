How to

1. Pick your sets

Tap ☆ on a set to star it ★. Tap again for 🌟 SUPERMUSTSEE. Use the day buttons, search and the "Show" filter to find sets.

2. Add your friends

Type your name, then My list → Export my list and send the file to your friends. Load theirs with + Add friend's list. Their picks show as coloured people icons on each set.

3. Party mode

Open PARTY MODE on your phone at the festival. It shows what's on now, where to head next, clashes to choose between, and who's going. Tap a date at the top to see that day. Until set times are out, it lists each day's picks by stage.

Works offline, no internet needed. Keep the party mode page loaded and it'll handle itself.

Party mode changes once set times are out: before that it can only list picks by stage. To see the full version, try the previous edition, which has set times: EDC Thailand Jan 2026, then its party mode mid-festival.

4. Take it with you

My list → Add to calendar puts your sets in your phone's calendar. 🖨️ gives a printable timetable.

Everything is saved in this browser only. Export your list to move it to another device.
